About Me

Building websites that grow businesses

I'm Abiodun Oluwafunmibi, a self-taught frontend developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, operating under the brand Funmibitech. I help small business owners get a proper online presence — fast, clean, and professional websites that make their business look credible and attract customers.

Beyond client work, I create tech content — tips, tutorials, and insights for developers and tech curious people across LinkedIn, TikTok, and Instagram. I also build every website with security best practices baked in, because a good website should be a safe one too.

How It Works

From First Call to Live Website

A clear, simple process so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

1

Discovery Call

We talk about your business, goals, and what you need. No jargon — just a simple, honest conversation.

2

Proposal & Plan

I send a clear proposal — scope, timeline, and price. You approve it, and we get started.

3

Design & Build

I design and develop your site with clean code, fast load times, and mobile-first layout throughout.

4

Review & Revise

You review the live preview and give feedback. We refine until you're completely happy with it.

5

Launch & Handoff

Your site goes live. You get the files, credentials, and a walkthrough so you're never left guessing.
