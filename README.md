## 📌 About

This portfolio is designed to give potential clients and employers a clear picture of who I am, what I build, and how to reach me. It features a dark hacker aesthetic with green accents and a clean, professional layout.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Home/Hero** — Introduction with a clear personal brand statement
- **Projects Page** — Alternating card layout showcasing 5 live projects
- **Testimonials Section** — Social proof from past clients
- **WhatsApp CTA** — Direct contact integration for quick client outreach
- **SEO Optimized** — Sitemap, meta tags, and Google Search Console verified
- **Fully Responsive** — Works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
